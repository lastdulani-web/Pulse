    About

    Version 1.1 · Developed by Last Dulani

    Privacy

    Your music never leaves this device. There are no accounts, no tracking and no uploads. Songs are copied into this app’s private storage and play offline.

    Getting started

    • Add music: Library, then Import songs or Import folder. On iPhone, tap Browse to reach On My iPhone or iCloud Drive.
    • Install: iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen. Android, menu then Install app. PC, the install icon in the address bar.
    • Lyrics: Pulse reads lyrics saved inside your songs, and synced .lrc files that share a song’s name. Import them together, or tap Add .lrc file on the lyrics view. To make your own, tap Sync on the lyrics view, paste your lyrics and tap along as the song plays. You can save the result to the song or export an .lrc file.
    • Formats: MP3, FLAC, WAV and M4A.
    • Offline: after the first load, Pulse opens with no internet.
